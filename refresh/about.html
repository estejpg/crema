hi, we're crema

a cafe for
slow mornings.

We opened our doors on Ventura Blvd with a simple idea: great drinks, made carefully, in a room that feels like a friend's kitchen.

the craft

small bar,
big standards.

Every espresso drink starts with a double shot pulled to order. Our chocolate and mocha sauces are made in house, our matcha is ceremonial-grade Nekohama, whisked in small batches, and our teas are steeped one cup at a time.

No shortcuts, no fuss, just drinks we're proud to hand across the counter.

the room

sunlight, wood,
and open windows.

Our nano windows fold all the way open, letting the morning in and the espresso smell out. Grab a seat at the counter, settle in with your laptop, or take a stroopwafel to go.

You'll spot our little mascot everywhere, on cups, on the cart, on the wall. Say hi.

the neighborhood

made in, by, and for
Sherman Oaks.

We're a neighborhood spot first. Regulars' orders memorized, dogs greeted by name, and a cart that rolls out for the block's best days.

Follow along at @crema.shermanoaks, or better, come by.
